StudyBuddy AI

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

StudyBuddy ("we", "our", or "app") is dedicated to protecting user privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when using the StudyBuddy mobile application.

1. Information We Collect and Process

  • Audio Data: When using the oral recall or voice challenge features, the app accesses your device microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) to capture spoken answers. Audio recordings are transmitted to our secure server solely for real-time speech evaluation and are not retained as permanent audio files.
  • Documents and Study Materials: The app allows you to upload textbook slices and question papers (PDF or text). These files are processed to extract relevant text chunks, index embeddings, and generate academic explanations.
  • Account and Authentication Data: We utilize Firebase Authentication to securely manage accounts. Basic identifiers such as your Firebase User ID and authentication tokens are used to isolate sessions and store academic vector indexes.

2. Use of Information

  • To evaluate oral answers and generate immediate academic feedback.
  • To retrieve relevant citations and answers from provided textbooks.
  • To safeguard session limits and enforce account security.

3. Data Storage and Deletion

Uploaded documents and temporary voice recordings are processed transiently. Users can clear session-related textbook vectors and data directly from within the app using the workspace controls.

4. Third-Party Services

The app relies on trusted third-party providers for infrastructure and machine learning:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase Authentication
  • Google ML Kit (On-device text recognition)

5. Security

All client-server communications are transmitted using secure transport protocols to protect personal study data in transit.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via your associated project repository on GitHub.
